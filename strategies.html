The instructional response

Two axes to diagnose.
Seven lenses to respond.

Knowledge type and learning stage locate the difficulty. The response is then chosen for that cell: 3–4 strategies, each tagged by the lens of practice it belongs to. A strategy is not good or bad in general, only right or wrong for a cell.
