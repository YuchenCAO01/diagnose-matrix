The matrix · Years 9–10

Knowledge × stage → response

Each cell is a different teaching problem. Click a cell for what you'll see, what success looks like, the strategies that fit, and the research behind it.

Rows adapt Anderson & Krathwohl's knowledge dimension, with conditional knowledge in the metacognitive row. Columns follow Haring & Eaton's instructional hierarchy. A topic can sit in different cells for different students.
